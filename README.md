## 🌟 About the Project

**Lithe Board** is an IoT Dashboard Platform that initially started as a personal project to pass the time, but it gradually evolved into something more meaningful. Over time, the project transformed into a platform aimed at providing a simpler, more accessible solution for those interested in IoT, especially for individuals with limited technical knowledge. The goal has always been to create an intuitive, customizable platform that meets the needs of users who may find other IoT solutions too complex or expensive. While there is still much to improve, with your help, **Lithe Board** has the potential to become a strong influence in the market, offering a more feature-rich and affordable alternative to existing platforms that are often limited in functionality and high in cost.
