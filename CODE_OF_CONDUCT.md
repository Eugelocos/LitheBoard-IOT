# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering an open and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of age, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, race, ethnicity, religion, level of experience, or any other personal characteristic.

As contributors and maintainers of this project, we pledge to treat all participants with respect and dignity.

---

## Standards of Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:
- Using inclusive and welcoming language.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Accepting constructive criticism with an open mind.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Using sexualized language or imagery, or any inappropriate conduct.
- Insulting, derogatory, or personal attacks.
- Engaging in any form of harassment or intimidation.

---

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying acceptable behavior and taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter behavior that you believe violates this Code of Conduct, please report it through the following channels:
- **Email:** [litheboard@gmail.com](mailto:litheboard@gmail.com)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated confidentially.
